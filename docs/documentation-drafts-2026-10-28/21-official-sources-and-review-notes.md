## Ghana data protection

- [Data Protection Commission: organisations](https://dpc.gov.gh/for-organisations/) and [compliance guidance](https://dpc.gov.gh/compliance/): review registration, privacy notices, accountable supervision and safeguards. Owner must confirm actual DPC registration status; do not invent a registration number.
- [Individuals' rights](https://dpc.gov.gh/for-individuals/) and [official contact](https://dpc.gov.gh/contact/): shape access/correction/objection and complaint routes with proportionate identity verification.
- [Data Protection Act 2012, Act 843, official NCA copy](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Data-Protection-Act-2012.pdf): review purpose/necessity, information, retention, security, processors, special personal data and security-compromise notification. Confirm the precise applicable sections and current guidance with an adviser. Avoid importing GDPR terminology, legal bases or a universal 72-hour rule without Ghana-specific analysis.

## Age and transaction terms

- [Children's Act 1998, Act 560, Parliament copy](https://ir.parliament.gh/bitstream/handle/123456789/1772/CHILDREN%E2%80%99S%20ACT%2C%201998%20%28ACT%20560%29.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1): the child definition is under 18. The owner's platform minimum of 16 does not resolve capacity to enter merchant, housing or payment arrangements. Review guardian authorization and children's data safeguards.
- [Electronic Transactions Act, government regulatory register](https://www.brr.gov.gh/search_detail.php?indexes_id=NTEx~Electronic+Transactions+Act,+2008+%28Act+772%29+as+amended+by+the+Electronic+Transactions+%28Amendment%29+Act,+2012+%28Act+838%29~79~57) and [official Act 772 PDF](https://nita.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/Electronic-Transactions-Act-772.pdf): review supplier disclosures (including identity, contact and full cost), access to/saveability of terms, correcting errors, delivery and the applicable cancellation/refund rules and exceptions. Do not state that every textbook, service, accommodation arrangement or started service has the same cancellation period. Check all relevant amendments.

## Provider and analytics references

- [Google Analytics SPA guidance](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/ga4/single-page-applications) and [setup](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9304153): correctly count virtual navigation without duplicate or sensitive page data.
- [Google Measurement ID location](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/12270356?hl=en): a web stream's public identifier starts with `G-`; it is not an account password or server secret.
- [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/), [DDoS protection](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ddos-protection/), [origin protection](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/security/protect-your-origin-server/) and [Web Analytics](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/): distinguish form checks, proxied filtering and analytics.
