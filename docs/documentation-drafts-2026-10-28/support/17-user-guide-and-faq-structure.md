## Starter FAQ wording

**Does a PineVela account reserve a room?** An account alone does not allocate housing. Use the authorized housing activation process and your separate accommodation agreement.

**Can anyone create a staff account?** No. Staff access is created/invited by an authorized manager for their assigned housing.

**Does PineVela hold the money for a marketplace handover?** The current physical-goods flow pays the seller directly at handover. Do not describe it as escrow.

**Why must I accept the Terms?** The app records the published version you accept. The Privacy Policy explains data handling; accepting Terms does not opt you into marketing or optional AI provider sharing.

**Why did an order disappear from active reservations?** Current reservations last 24 hours and can expire or be cancelled. Check order history and the latest state before handover.

**Can support restore my encrypted message history without my recovery information?** Do not promise this. Follow the app's encryption recovery process; support cannot recreate a private recovery phrase.

**Does signing out delete my account?** No. Signing out ends the app session. Account/context closure and a personal-data erasure request are separate processes, and some records may need justified retention.

**How does System default work?** It follows the browser/phone's theme, including later changes. Explicit Light or Dark uses the saved choice instead. The public landing switch has Light/Dark only.

Avoid FAQ promises that every property is risk-free, every merchant is honest, all messages are encrypted including support, a refund is automatic, or an app alert is emergency assistance. Link approved policy text instead of creating contradictory answers.
