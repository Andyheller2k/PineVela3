# Onboarding and support copy library

Status: DRAFT COPY. Align all wording with the released app and approved policies before use.

## Registration and agreement

Signup checkbox: “I am at least 16 and I have read and accept the Terms of service (version [published version]).” Link the version-specific agreement; leave the checkbox unchecked.

Privacy helper: “See the Privacy Policy for how your data is used. This does not opt you into marketing or AI sharing.”

Existing-account screen: “Read our Terms of service before using your account. We’ll save the version you accept so you can continue without this step next time.” Buttons: “Accept and continue”, “Sign out”. Keep support and account-closure contact available.

Merchant declaration: “I certify that I am an enrolled student at this university and that these details and identification are genuine.” Show the separate Terms control immediately before submission. Once approved merchant-specific terms are published, name and link that agreement explicitly too.

Version error: “The Terms of service changed. Reload and read the current version before accepting.” Missing agreement: “Read and accept the Terms of service to continue.” Never silently tick a checkbox or write acceptance when merely opening a policy.

## Feature notices

Email verification: “Enter the code sent to your email. Check spam if it does not arrive. Never share it with another person.” Show the real resend cooldown.

Merchant review: “Your verified application has been submitted for review. This is not permission to trade yet.” Rejection: “Review the administrator's notes, correct your details and submit again.” Do not guarantee a review deadline until operations approves one.

Direct handover: “Inspect the item and confirm payment directly with the seller before completing the exchange. PineVela does not hold escrow funds in this flow.”

Message recovery: “Keep your recovery information private. You may need it to unlock your encrypted conversation history on another device.” Do not ask the user to send it to support.

Optional AI: Name the **actual selected provider**, disclose which prompt/context is sent, request the specific supported confirmation and provide cancel/decline. Generic Terms acceptance is not this confirmation.

## Support response templates

Acknowledgement: “We received your request [reference]. Please send the relevant order/account reference and a brief description. Do not send passwords, verification codes, recovery phrases or payment PINs. [Approved response target and next step].”

Privacy request: “We received your request concerning [scope]. We may ask for proportionate information to confirm your authority. We will explain the action taken and any applicable retention limits. [Verified contact and lawful response process].”

Payment investigation: “Please provide the payment reference, date and amount. We will check the applicable platform transaction. If you paid a seller directly, include the order reference so the issue can be directed to the responsible seller.”

Appeal acknowledgement: “We received your request to review [decision/reference]. Tell us what you believe is incorrect and include relevant new evidence. [Actual review route and target].”

Service incident: “We are investigating [confirmed issue]. [Affected feature] may be unavailable. [Verified workaround and next update].” Do not speculate about a breach or promise a resolution time without evidence.
