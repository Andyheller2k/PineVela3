## What your account does

PineVela gives an authorized resident access to the housing features associated with their active allocation. General platform access is 16+, subject to any guardian permission or higher age required for a particular arrangement. Room activation does not itself create a tenancy, confirm legal ownership of a property or prove a rent payment.

Your separate accommodation agreement identifies your housing provider, premises, charges, duration, deposit, repair responsibilities, house rules, termination and dispute process. `[FILL: how residents obtain that agreement and the housing operator's contact.]`

## Account activation and allocation

Use your own verified account and the authorized activation process. Keep room keys, passwords, verification codes and recovery information private. A resident may have only one active allocation. Ask the authorized housing operator to correct an error; do not activate another person's room or reuse a key after closure.

## Maintenance, notices and reviews

Report maintenance through the relevant housing workflow and include only information needed to address the problem. For immediate danger use the appropriate local emergency service or housing emergency contact: `[FILL: verified property-specific emergency contact]`. Do not rely on an app notification as an emergency dispatch guarantee.

Resident-to-staff reviews are limited to eligible activated housing relationships. Maintenance repair ratings are separate. Give honest, relevant feedback without threats, discriminatory abuse or private information. These rules do not create public staff ratings or general housing chat.

## Privacy, leaving and complaints

Residents' account, allocation and maintenance information is available only to parties with the applicable authorized role and purpose. Read the Privacy Policy. Ask support about access or correction and the housing operator about contractual accommodation issues.
