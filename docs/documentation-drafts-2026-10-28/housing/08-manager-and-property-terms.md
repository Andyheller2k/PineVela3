# Housing manager and property terms — drafting template

Status: UNPUBLISHED DRAFT. Version/effective date: `[FILL]`. Operator: PineVela Ghana; pinevela@pinevela.app.

## Authority and verification

You must be authorized to represent each property you submit and manage. Provide accurate contact, property, licence and authority information requested by the actual verification process. PineVela may require review and inspection before approval. `[FILL: evidence required, inspection scope, renewal/reinspection and who may approve a property.]` Approval describes those checks, not a guarantee of title, legal compliance, personal safety or uninterrupted utilities.

Manage only properties assigned to your account. A client-supplied property number, room key or another person's credentials does not establish authority. Correct material changes promptly; never publish another operator's property without permission.

## Property information and housing arrangements

Keep advertised facilities, location, availability, pricing, rules and contact details accurate. Disclose total housing charges, deposits, payment recipient, occupancy terms and relevant restrictions through the actual offer. `[FILL: whether PineVela provides only management/listing tools or participates in a separately approved booking process.]`

Provide a separate legally reviewed tenancy, licence or accommodation agreement where required. It should identify the housing parties and premises, term, charges, deposits, repair duties, house rules, termination, lawful access and dispute process. App activation does not replace that agreement.

## Allocations and operations

Respect authorized allocations and the single-active-allocation rule. The architecture supports a Hostel → Block → Floor → Room → Bed hierarchy, but the currently documented activation workflow is room-based; do not promise bed selection or hotel booking functions that have not shipped. Record transfers, closures and corrections through supported workflows rather than sharing keys between unrelated residents.

Handle maintenance requests and repair records appropriately. `[FILL: service targets, emergency contact, escalation and safety responsibilities.]` PineVela is not an emergency response service.

## Staff and resident information

Only an authorized manager may create or invite staff for their assigned housing. Staff access is not public recruitment. Give each staff member an individual authorized account and remove access when duties end. Do not use PineVela data for unrelated marketing or disclose residents' identity, allocation, contact or support records to unauthorized people.

Document each party's privacy responsibilities. `[REVIEW: independent-controller versus processor relationships by purpose; do not assume a single data-processing role for all housing activities.]` Use a separate processing or data-sharing agreement if needed, with purpose, instructions, confidentiality, security, sub-processors, rights assistance, incident notification and end-of-service arrangements.

## Reviews, communications and ending access

Eligible resident-to-staff reviews require an activated housing relationship; do not solicit public staff ratings. Maintenance repair ratings are a different workflow. Use supported notices, support and maintenance tools; these terms do not introduce manager–resident, manager–staff or room chat.

Property or manager closure follows the app's reviewed lifecycle and may retain operational history, payment references and legal records. `[FILL: export requests, notice, access handover and retention terms.]` Do not promise immediate physical deletion of every record or backup.
