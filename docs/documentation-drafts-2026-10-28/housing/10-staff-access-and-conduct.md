## Access and role

Your access is created or invited only by an authorized manager for their assigned housing. It is limited to your assigned role, property and duties. PineVela access is not an employment offer or employment contract. `[FILL: employer/operator, job terms handled outside PineVela and manager contact.]`

Use your own verified identity and required access controls. Do not share credentials, accept another worker's session, change ownership fields or attempt to enter another hostel's records. Notify the manager when your assignment changes or ends.

## Information handling

Access resident, maintenance and housing information only for authorized duties. Keep it confidential; do not copy it into personal contact lists, post it publicly or use it for unrelated sales. Use approved devices and channels. Do not ask residents for their passwords, banking PINs or recovery phrases.

`[FILL: permitted work devices, device lock rules, exports, lost-device reporting and record handling.]` These procedures should match the actual tools available rather than require unsupported app functions.

## Conduct, maintenance and reviews

Record work accurately and escalate unresolved safety or maintenance issues to the responsible manager. Do not falsify completion, retaliate against a reviewer or pressure residents to submit positive feedback. Eligible resident-to-staff reviews are tied to activated housing relationships. Repair ratings remain a separate maintenance process.

There is no public staff hiring, public staff rating directory, manager–staff chat or room messaging feature under these terms. Use the actual authorized work and support channels.

## Ending access and incidents

The manager must use the authorized account lifecycle when duties end. Logging out is not the same as removing an assignment. Report suspected misuse, wrong-property access or a lost device promptly to `[FILL: monitored security/manager contact]`. Do not investigate by opening unrelated residents' records.
