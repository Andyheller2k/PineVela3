## 4. Google sign-in

When you choose Google sign-in, PineVela requests openid, email and profile information. The flow supplies an account identifier and basic verified profile information, which may include a name and profile image. It is used for account creation/linking, sign-in and relevant profile details.

This integration does not request Gmail, Drive, calendar or contact access. You can review your connection in your Google Account. Revoking the connection does not itself delete your PineVela account or stored transaction records.

Optional AI uses the provider identified in the feature; OpenAI moderation is used where the relevant moderation integration is enabled. Maps use the configured tile, place-search and routing providers and may open external map services.

### Optional device notifications

When enabled, Android alerts use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and browser alerts use the browser's push provider. PineVela stores the device token/subscription, a device identifier, verified account bindings and notification choices. Private alerts contain an account label and short summary; optional marketplace/residence discovery alerts can include public listing details and pictures. Keys, payment PINs and encrypted message contents are not included in device payloads.

Each account connects only after it has been authenticated on that installation. Separate opt-in controls cover marketplace and residence discovery. Disabling alerts or signing out disconnects that device. Registration leases expire after 90 days without renewal and are removed after another 30 days; delivery-job records are removed after 30 days. Existing in-app notification history has its own retention. [REVIEW: confirm provider contracts, lock-screen guidance and deployed cleanup behaviour.]

## 6. Cookies, security and analytics

Essential cookies and browser storage support sessions, security, saved preferences and installed-app functions. Cloudflare Turnstile processes browser/connection signals for applicable authentication checks. The Cookies and Analytics Notice identifies the current categories and available controls.

An earlier 28 October check found no visitor tracker. The subsequent owner-authorized release adds optional Google Analytics 4 (`G-NWBP5E04F9`), pending manual installation and first live verification. The tag loads after a separate Allow analytics choice and can be turned off through the website footer, Privacy Policy or app settings. Terms acceptance does not enable analytics. General page names and normalized store paths are used; account identifiers, private form/message contents, sensitive URLs and full referrer queries are excluded. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. Analytics cookies are configured for up to 30 days without extending their expiry on each visit. `[FILL: approved lawful basis, GA4 property retention/access settings and confirmed deployment date.]`

## 7. Messages, reports and AI

The implemented new buyer–seller messaging flow uses end-to-end encryption. Legacy pre-encryption history and operational metadata have different treatment. Support communications remain available to authorized support personnel. A participant can intentionally disclose a selected message when reporting it; a recipient can also copy material it can read. Do not promise that every communication or all metadata is end-to-end encrypted.

Optional AI identifies the selected provider before the confirmation to enable replies. Content sent can include the submitted message and the limited context described in that feature. Avoid sending passwords, recovery phrases, room keys, payment PINs or unnecessary personal details. `[FILL: actual provider, context fields, moderation disclosures and retention.]`
