## Draft process

Tell us what happened, when, the relevant reference and the outcome you request. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your authority or understand the issue. Never send passwords, full bank/card credentials, room activation secrets, AI provider keys or recovery phrases.

We aim to acknowledge within `[FILL: achievable target]` and provide a substantive response within `[FILL]`. If more time is needed, we will explain the next step where appropriate. These targets do not replace an applicable legal deadline.

An authorized reviewer will consider relevant records, supplied evidence and the applicable policy. A report is not itself proof of wrongdoing. We may take urgent protective action where necessary and explain the review/appeal route subject to privacy, security and legal constraints.
