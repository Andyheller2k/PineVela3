# Acceptable use and content rules — drafting template

Status: UNPUBLISHED DRAFT. Version/effective date: `[FILL]`. Applies to all supported roles.

## Use the service honestly

Do not impersonate another person, submit false student or property evidence, misrepresent ownership or share credentials. Do not access another account, residence, allocation, store, document or private message without authorization. Do not evade bans or abuse registration, verification, reviews, payments or support.

Do not attempt credential theft, malicious uploads, denial-of-service attacks, security-control bypass, unsolicited bulk messages or scraping that exposes private information. `[FILL: responsible vulnerability-report contact and authorized research process.]` Do not test another user's account to demonstrate a problem.

## Content and listings

Post only content you may lawfully use and disclose. Respect privacy, copyright, trade marks and other rights. Describe products, services, property and qualifications accurately. Do not publish identity documents, private contacts or message extracts without an appropriate basis.

Prohibit unlawful goods/services, fraud, stolen or counterfeit items, sexual exploitation, threats, harassment and discriminatory abuse. `[DECIDE: a category-specific prohibited-items schedule covering regulated medicines, weapons, alcohol/tobacco, financial services and other categories that the platform cannot lawfully support.]` Obtain Ghana-specific review rather than treating this example list as an exhaustive legal classification.

## Reviews and communications

No fake orders, review swapping, bought ratings, undisclosed incentives or retaliatory threats. Use only review functions for which the account is eligible. Resident-to-staff reviews require the active housing relationship; public staff ratings are not supported. Maintenance repair ratings are separate.

Buyer–seller messaging supports marketplace activity. Do not introduce general chat, room DMs or manager–resident/staff chat through a generic messaging interpretation of these rules.

## Moderation and reports

PineVela may check supported submitted content automatically and through authorized human review. Approval does not certify legality, authenticity or safety. Reports can intentionally share selected evidence with reviewers; do not upload unrelated private material.

A claim of copyright or other infringement should identify the work/right, location of the disputed content, claimant contact, authority and an accurate explanation; adapt the process to applicable law rather than copying a US-only statutory notice.
