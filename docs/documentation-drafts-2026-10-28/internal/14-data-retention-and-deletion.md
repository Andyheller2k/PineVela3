## Request handling

1. Log scope and verify the requester proportionately; never ask for a password.
2. Distinguish signing out, closing one role/store/room and full personal-data erasure.
3. Map live systems, subprocessors, media, retained business records and backups.
4. Identify any justified retention/hold, responsible party and release date.
5. Perform supported authorized actions, document what was erased/restricted and explain lawful exceptions.
6. Notify relevant recipients/processors where required; retain minimal request evidence and review the outcome.

Current lifecycle tools preserve some history and payment references. Do not promise instant erasure across every table, recipient, participant device and backup. Add automated retention jobs only after approval, scoped previews, tests and recovery planning. This pack does not authorize a bulk deletion job.
