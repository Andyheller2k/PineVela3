## Shared prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high fidelity mobile web authentication concept
board for PineVela, a student housing and marketplace app. Present two complete
mobile screens side by side at equal scale: Login and Sign Up. Use the same
design language and header artwork for both. Keep screens large, legible and
front-facing, without hands, hardware chrome or perspective. Give each board
its design number and name on a restrained neutral background.

Reuse the exact existing girl artwork as an upper visual header. Preserve her
recognizable appearance, keep her face unobscured and fit or crop the lower
beanbag tastefully without distorting the figure. The form must be below the
artwork, with no picture behind the input fields. Use navy #001D3D, blue
#003566, yellow #FFC300 and #FFD60A, contemporary rounded sans-serif typography,
the wordmark “PineVela”, readable labels, subtle line icons and realistic input
and button targets. Fit each complete screen naturally.

Login content: “Welcome back”, short supporting copy, Login / Sign Up segmented
control with Login selected, “Username or email”, “Password”, “Forgot password?”,
primary “Login”, secondary “Continue with Google”, and “New to PineVela? Sign Up”.

Sign Up content: “Create your account”, same segmented control with Sign Up
selected, “Full name”, “Email address”, “Password”, “Confirm password”, primary
“Create Account”, secondary “Continue with Google”, and “Already a member? Login”.
Use a compact two-column password row if needed. Avoid extra fields, invented
features, face-obscuring cards, tiny controls, illegible decorative text,
excessive empty space and a form superimposed on the woman.

## Per-design directions

1. **Soft curve:** Navy/blue hero in the upper roughly 36%, authentic warm
   artwork colors, shallow concave wave with thin yellow contour into an
   integrated deep navy lower form. Yellow headings and pale labels; restrained
   and directly buildable.
2. **Light curve:** Navy/blue upper hero, pronounced smooth curved boundary and
   narrow gold accent into an ivory/pale blue lower form surface. Navy text and
   outlines, yellow actions and a navy selected segment; bright and clean.
3. **Golden arch:** Broad symmetrical architectural curve below a navy hero.
   Muted warm gold lower surroundings containing an inset rounded navy form
   card, with gold actions and pale text. Keep the form entirely below artwork.
4. **Layered wave:** Asymmetric S-shaped yellow and offset blue ribbon curves
   connecting the upper artwork to a lower navy section. Put a readable glass
   form card entirely below the wave; keep the ribbon compact and tidy.
5. **Blur transition:** No hard curve or drawn divider. Sharp upper girl artwork
   dissolves through a shallow, softly blurred navy gradient into the lower navy
   form. Keep face and upper body sharp, fade around the lower beanbag, use
   legible glass fields and gold accents, and avoid fog over text. This soft
   separation should distinguish it clearly from options 1–4.
