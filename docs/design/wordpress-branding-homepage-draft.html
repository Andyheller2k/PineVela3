PineVela

Housing management and a student marketplace, in one place.

PineVela helps residents, housing managers, staff and sellers keep housing records and marketplace activity organized. Residents can activate an assigned room, manage their profile and report maintenance needs. Authorized managers and staff use the housing tools for the properties they manage.

A marketplace for campus life

Explore published stores and listings, save products, place purchase requests and follow your orders. Buyers and sellers can communicate about marketplace transactions through the app.

Sign in your way

Create an account with email or choose Google sign-in. Google sign-in uses your basic account identity to create or access your PineVela account.
