1. About PineVela

PineVela is operated by Oppong Manu Andy, Ghana ("we", "us" or "our"). PineVela provides housing-management and marketplace services for residents, housing managers, staff, sellers and administrators at pinevela.app. This policy describes how we handle information through those services. Contact andyheller2k@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

PineVela is intended for people aged 16 or older. Users aged 16 or 17 need a parent or guardian's permission where required by applicable law. If you believe someone under 16 has provided personal information through PineVela, contact us so we can review the account and take appropriate action.

2. Information we handle

  • Account information: names, usernames, email addresses, phone numbers, chosen or uploaded profile images, sign-in details and account preferences.
  • Housing information: registrations, housing assignments, room activation, staff relationships, property addresses and map coordinates, maintenance requests and reviews, and documents or photographs submitted for an applicable housing or verification process.
  • Marketplace information: seller and store profiles, listings, uploaded content, saved items, orders, fulfilment details and payment or transaction records associated with services used.
  • Communications: buyer–seller conversations, support requests, safety reports and information voluntarily entered into optional AI features.
  • Technical information: session identifiers, browser preferences and operational or security records needed to deliver and protect the service. Hosting and delivery providers may process connection information, including IP addresses.

The information required depends on the features and account roles you use. Avoid including unrelated personal or sensitive information in uploads, messages or AI prompts.

3. Signing in with Google

Google sign-in is optional. When you choose it, PineVela requests the openid, email and profile permissions. Google and our authentication provider supply an account identifier, verified email address, name and basic profile information, which may include a profile photo.

PineVela uses this information to verify your identity, create or connect your PineVela account, and maintain your account details. Authentication information is processed through Supabase Auth and the PineVela service. Relevant account details may appear in the housing or marketplace relationships you choose to use.

This sign-in flow does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts or calendars. You can review or revoke the connection in your Google Account connections. Revoking Google access does not by itself delete a PineVela account or its records.

4. How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, manage authorized housing relationships, operate marketplace listings and orders, deliver notifications and support, check submitted content, investigate misuse and maintain service security. Information submitted for a particular feature is handled for that feature and its associated operational, support and security needs.

Session cookies and browser storage support sign-in, security, display preferences and installed-app functionality. Signing out ends the current app session; clearing browser storage does not erase records held by the service.

Where enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile processes browser and connection signals and security-check results to help prevent automated abuse during authentication. This security check is separate from the optional Ask AI feature and its confirmation.

5. Who receives information

Relevant information is available to participants and authorized operators involved in your use of the service, such as your housing manager, a seller fulfilling an order, a buyer contacting your store, or support and safety reviewers. Public store and listing content is visible to marketplace visitors.

Service providers process information needed to operate PineVela. These include Supabase for authentication, database and file storage, Spaceship for application hosting, Resend for supported service emails, and the authentication provider's configured email delivery service. AI recipients are described below. Providers may process information outside Ghana or your country.

Payments: Paystack receives transaction information, such as an email address, amount and payment reference, and processes payment details for supported payment methods. Mobile-money payments may require a phone number and provider. The payment provider's own terms and privacy information also apply.

Maps: map tiles, place search and directions use OpenStreetMap-related services, Nominatim and Valhalla. The relevant searches, map requests and coordinates are shared with the selected service. Device location is used when you grant browser permission. Choosing an external Google Maps link opens Google's service.

New buyer–seller messages use end-to-end encryption. Earlier messages sent before this feature was introduced remain labelled as legacy history and are not retroactively end-to-end encrypted. Message delivery and account relationships still require service records. A participant can choose to disclose a selected message to support through a report; encryption does not prevent another participant from copying or sharing content they can read.

Optional Google Analytics

If you allow optional analytics, PineVela uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits, general page use, traffic sources, device/browser information and approximate geographic activity. Google receives the network information needed for those requests. We send general page names instead of private account identifiers, raw search terms, form entries, message contents or authentication/payment URLs. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in this integration.

The Google tag loads only after you choose Allow analytics. You can decline and continue using PineVela. Your choice is remembered on this browser; accepting the Terms of service does not enable analytics. Analytics cookies are configured for up to 30 days without extending their expiry on each visit. Google Analytics property retention settings are managed separately.

Change analytics preferences here, in the website footer or in the app's settings. Turning analytics off stops further collection by this integration and clears its accessible analytics cookies; it does not automatically remove data previously received by Google. Google's Privacy Policy also applies to its handling of information. Contact us about a privacy request.

6. Optional AI and content moderation

Ask AI: AI replies require your explicit confirmation before content is sent to the named provider. Depending on the service configuration, the provider is Cloudflare Workers AI or Groq; the feature identifies the selected recipient before you enable replies. Requests may include your message, first name, optional interests, limited recent conversation context, and public listing or notification-count summaries used to answer your question.

You can turn AI replies off and clear the saved conversation. The assistant cannot read encrypted buyer–seller chats or retrieve your security PIN. Avoid entering passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary personal details.

Content safety: new uploaded profile pictures, marketplace photographs, store artwork, listing text and store-profile text are checked using OpenAI's moderation service before publication. The submitted image or text is sent for this check; content can itself contain personal information. Flagged submissions may be retained for review by authorized safety operators. Private housing, inspection and maintenance photographs, identity documents and encrypted buyer–seller chats are outside this moderation flow.

7. Storage, retention and security

Account and service records are stored using access-controlled systems. Retention depends on the record's purpose, account status, transaction history, safety review, backup handling and applicable obligations. Closing a housing or seller context does not automatically erase historical payment, housing, support or governance records.

Ask AI displays a limited recent history. Older entries can remain stored until later cleanup; an inactive conversation is not guaranteed to be automatically erased after a fixed number of days. The Clear conversation control deletes the account's saved AI conversation, while preferences and applicable usage records remain separate.

Contact us to request deletion or to ask about a particular record's retention. We assess which records can be deleted and which are still needed for an active service, transaction, safety process or applicable legal obligation. Records in provider backups may remain until the relevant backup is replaced or expires.

8. Your choices and requests

You can update available profile settings, choose whether to use Google sign-in, control optional AI replies, clear saved AI conversations, manage device-location permission and sign out. Some records cannot be changed directly because they form part of a transaction, housing history or safety process.

To request access, correction or deletion of personal information, raise an objection to its use, or ask about your rights under applicable data-protection law, email andyheller2k@gmail.com. Identity verification and an assessment of retained records may be needed. PineVela does not currently offer an unrestricted, instant account-wide deletion control.

You may also contact Ghana's Data Protection Commission about a data-protection concern.

9. Changes and contact

We update this policy when the service or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through the service or other appropriate notices, including a fresh confirmation where a changed optional feature requires it.
